The flaw in the 18.5 kWh/100 km trip computer

When an EV trip consumes 24 kWh/100 km instead of the rated 17, typical car dashboards blame the driver. They provide zero visibility into whether the loss was caused by high aerodynamic drag from a 40 km/h headwind, cold battery preconditioning, sub-zero cabin heating, tire rolling resistance, or 800 meters of vertical mountain climb.

Aerodynamic Drag F_aero
P_aero = ½ · ρ(T) · C_d · A · v³

Scales with the cube of vehicle velocity. Exergy applies continuous ambient air density corrections based on barometric pressure and outside temperature, allowing drivers to isolate headwind effects from speed increases.

Climate HVAC Conditioning P_hvac
P_hvac = Q_thermal / COP(T_ext, T_cabin)

Calculates the thermal coefficient of performance (COP) of the vehicle's heat pump or PTC heater. Isolates cabin conditioning from battery thermal preconditioning, proving exactly how much winter range is lost to heating.

Gravitational Elevation Work ΔE_pot
P_climb = m · g · v · sin(θ)

Attributes energy spent lifting the vehicle's mass against gravity versus potential energy stored. Separates grade climb consumption from rolling drag and calculates true round-trip descent recovery.

Regenerative Recovery η_regen
E_rec = η_regen · ∫ (m · a_decel · v) dt

Directly tracks kilowatt-hours recovered back into the high-voltage pack during blended friction or one-pedal deceleration. Reports mechanical-to-chemical conversion efficiency and warns when cold battery limits braking regen.

12V Auxiliary Systems P_aux
P_aux = V_12v · I_12v + P_infotainment

Monitors the DC-DC converter output feeding vehicle ECUs, driver assist radars, exterior lighting, sound system, and displays. Reveals parasitic consumption while driving and in stationary stop-and-go traffic.

Standby & Vampire Loss P_standby
E_vampire = ∫_parked (P_bms + P_telematics) dt

Tracks high-voltage battery loss when the car is parked, asleep, or running battery thermal protection in freezing temperatures. Provides verified overnight percentage loss without guessing.

Local-first by construction. Zero account required.

Most connected car apps upload raw, unencrypted GPS tracks to corporate clouds to profile drivers and resell telemetry. Exergy is built on a strict zero-knowledge foundation: the vehicle is the primary data sovereign, pairing is purely optical, and sync payloads are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before leaving the car.

1. Zero Cloud Account

The in-vehicle app works completely without registration, email address, password, or cloud profile. The internal flash storage on your car holds the authoritative, full-resolution database.

2. Optical QR Pairing

Pairing generates an ephemeral 256-bit symmetric pre-shared key (PSK) and Ed25519 keypair inside the vehicle in park. Your phone camera reads the optical QR code directly. No account server is ever involved in key exchange.

3. XChaCha20-Poly1305 E2EE

All sync payloads are encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 (192-bit extended nonce). Hardware-backed keys are held strictly in Android Keystore / Apple Keychain.

4. Blind Mailbox Relay

When cloud sync is enabled, the relay server acts as an untrusted blind mailbox. The server cannot inspect routes, speeds, energy usage, timestamps, or vehicle VIN, and automatically purges blobs after delivery.

5. In-Cabin Offline Wi-Fi

Reject the cloud completely: when your companion phone connects to the in-vehicle Wi-Fi hotspot, Exergy discovers the vehicle via mDNS and syncs over pinned TLS 1.3. Zero bytes leave the car cabin.

6. Open Data Export

Your data belongs to you. Export complete 1 Hz telemetry or aggregated summaries at any time to standard CSV, structured JSON Schema, or GPX tracks with custom power extensions.
